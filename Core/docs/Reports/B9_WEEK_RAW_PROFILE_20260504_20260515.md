## 4. Requêtes read-only recommandées

### 4.1 Coverage par jour

```sql
SELECT
  substr(ts_utc, 1, 10) AS day,
  COUNT(*) AS raw_tick_count_raw,
  COUNT(DISTINCT ts_utc || '|' || bid || '|' || ask || '|' || mid || '|' || spread) AS raw_tick_count_dedup,
  COUNT(*) - COUNT(DISTINCT ts_utc || '|' || bid || '|' || ask || '|' || mid || '|' || spread) AS raw_duplicate_count,
  ROUND(
    1.0 * (COUNT(*) - COUNT(DISTINCT ts_utc || '|' || bid || '|' || ask || '|' || mid || '|' || spread)) / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0),
    6
  ) AS raw_duplicate_ratio
FROM tick_stream
WHERE symbol = 'GBPUSD'
  AND substr(ts_utc, 1, 10) BETWEEN '2026-05-04' AND '2026-05-15'
GROUP BY substr(ts_utc, 1, 10)
ORDER BY day;
```

### 4.2 Lecture raw dédupliquée pour B9

```sql
SELECT DISTINCT
  ts_utc,
  bid,
  ask,
  mid,
  spread
FROM tick_stream
WHERE symbol = 'GBPUSD'
  AND ts_utc >= :start_utc
  AND ts_utc < :end_utc
ORDER BY ts_utc;
```
